Open Road Foundation

We fund one West Chester student a year to go to the school that fits them best.

1 in 8

Median household income in the West Chester Area School District is $137,133. That is exactly why the need goes unseen. About one in eight of the district's 12,042 students is economically disadvantaged.

100% of every donation goes into the Fund.

The founders pay for running the foundation, the fundraising and the administration behind an award. Nothing is deducted from what you give.

The school district chooses with us.

The district's scholarship committee and the founders read every application together. Neither picks alone.
